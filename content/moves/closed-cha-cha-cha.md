## Description

## Steps

## Hold/hand position

Closed hold.

## Lead

- When your left hand is extended, grab her shoulder blade.
- Give her the signal for a turn by raising your left arm and push, and raising the right arm.
- Continue cha cha one more time.
- Spin her back.

## Notes/variations

Also performable in open hold — see Open Cha Cha Cha.
